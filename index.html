Earth Camino

Listening to the Cry of the Earth

Six days, about 220 km and two stages a day. Cecily Maher is walking from Waterford City to Dublin, along the River Barrow and the Grand Canal, to arrive in time for the Climate March.

The walk is a way of listening. For six days Cecily travels at the pace of the rivers and canals, through the farmland, bogs and towns that depend on them, and pays attention to what the land is saying. She is walking for climate action, to carry that attention all the way to Dublin and to add one more pair of feet to the march.
