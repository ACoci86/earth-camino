About the Walk

On Monday 9 November, Cecily Maher will set off on a six-day walk from Waterford to Dublin to reach the National Climate March on Saturday 14 November.

During this time, Cecily will walk more than 220kms, over five marathons and traverse five counties. The Earth Camino will take in the beautiful South East Greenway to New Ross, the Barrow Way to Kildare, and the Grand Canal to Dublin City.

Cecily is a volunteer with the B Corridor Waterford, the Eco-Action Group of St Joseph's, St Benildus' & St Mary's Parish, and is a Laudato Si Animator. As a course facilitator and retreat provider, her work focuses on healing any sense of separation between humanity and the Earth.

Why she is walking

  • To slow to the rhythm of nature
  • Listen to the cry of the Earth
  • Raise awareness of the climate crisis
  • Solidarity with the National Climate March

She would be delighted for you to join her along the route. Come for a few steps, a kilometre, a stage or a day. All are very welcome!

The journey

Waterford to Dublin

The route follows the South East Greenway to New Ross, the Barrow north through Carlow and Kildare, joins the Grand Canal near Monasterevin and Rathangan, and ends at the Garden of Remembrance on Parnell Square, before the walk becomes part of the march.

The Peace of Wild Things

Wendell Berry

When despair for the world grows in me and I wake in the night at the least sound in fear of what my life and my children’s lives may be, I go and lie down where the wood drake rests in his beauty on the water, and the great heron feeds. I come into the peace of wild things who do not tax their lives with forethought of grief. I come into the presence of still water. And I feel above me the day-blind stars waiting with their light. For a time I rest in the grace of the world, and am free.
